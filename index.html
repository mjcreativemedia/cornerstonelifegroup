Is Cornerstone Life Group a lender?+

No. We operate as a business funding broker and referral partner. Funding providers make their own approval decisions and determine any offer, terms, and requirements.

What types of funding can I explore?+

Start by telling us your intended use, such as equipment, working capital, inventory, or expansion. Carlos can discuss potential provider options and their availability for your situation. Specific products are not guaranteed.

Can a newer business inquire?+

Yes, you can submit an inquiry, including if you are pre-launch. Time in business, revenue, location, and other factors affect what may be available. Submitting an inquiry does not establish eligibility.

Does this inquiry involve a credit check?+

This form collects introductory business and contact details. It does not request a credit check or authorize one. A provider may have separate credit review and authorization requirements if you proceed with an application.

What will I need, and how long does it take?+

You do not need to upload documents for this inquiry. A provider may later request business records, bank statements, identification, or other materials through its application process. Timing depends on the provider, product, and completeness of your application.

What about fees, repayment, or guarantees?+

Costs, payment schedules, collateral, and personal guarantee requirements depend on the product and provider. Request and review the complete terms, including any broker or referral fees, before accepting an offer.

Who receives my information?+

Your inquiry is sent to Cornerstone Life Group using our website’s security and email delivery services. If you pursue a provider option, Carlos will discuss the next steps and any information needed. Read our for more details.
